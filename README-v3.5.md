# Veritas Theology & Apologetics — v3.5

## New: Logic & Transcendental Apologetics

A standalone, fully client-side React/TypeScript module integrated into the existing sidebar. It includes:

- 29 searchable logical fallacies, categorized and presented with definitions, examples, diagnoses, response strategies, and qualifications where needed.
- Three classical laws of thought, a separately identified philosophical principle of sufficient reason, six deductive inference forms, and four reasoning types.
- Eleven substantial studies of the transcendental argument for God (TAG), covering its thesis, logic, knowledge, morality, induction, internal critique, Van Til, Bahnsen, Bavinck, major objections, and a worked dialogue.
- Explicitly evaluates a schematic TAG's validity and its disputed necessity premise; avoids asserting that non-Christians cannot reason.
- Ten interactive, curated quiz questions with immediate explanations and progress tracking within the session.
- Per-topic local-browser research notes and a JSON notes export. No AI integration, tracking, or network API required.
- Reference links to the 1689 Baptist Confession and academic discussions of fallacies, classical logic, and transcendental arguments.

## Deployment

Unzip the project **contents** at the root of your GitHub repository (not inside a second `veritas` folder). Netlify: `npm run build`; publish directory: `dist`. Existing features and browser-local notes are retained.

## Editorial commitments

Scripture is the ultimate theological authority. The 1689 Baptist Confession is a subordinate confessional standard. Roman Catholic and other non-Reformed views remain comparative study materials, not endorsements. Historical and philosophical sources are identified as such. TAG is argued as a Reformed presuppositional position; disputed premises and counterarguments are not concealed.
