# Veritas Reformata v3.21

Torah and Qur'an page, phase 1 of the comparative guide.

- Parallels index: 66 Bible and Qur'an parallels, Genesis to Deuteronomy, filterable by book and status (shared, differs, thematic only, no equivalent). Tap a row for both passages in English; optional Arabic. Rows with a full account link to it.
- Full offline English text: whole Pentateuch (KJV) and whole Qur'an (Pickthall 1930), both public domain (public/corpus/kjv-pentateuch.json, pickthall.json). Arabic is the Tanzil text already bundled.
- Doctrines tab: 8 theological differences with a Reformed reading and 1689 references.
- Qur'an on Scripture tab: 12 passages on the Torah and Gospel, with the pressure point.
- Loose links (Korah, Balaam, red heifer, Ishmael's birth, water from the rock) are labelled thematic.
Not yet built: Hebrew text, word-by-word grammar, Jewish and Islamic commentary.
