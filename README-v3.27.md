# Veritas Reformata v3.27
- Torah and Qur'anic Accounts: 18 contradiction cards folded into the accounts they belong to (Moses, Saul and Goliath, Joseph, Abraham, Adam, Aaron, Solomon, Mary). An index of all cards sits at the top of the Accounts tab, and each account in the list shows how many it has.
- Each card: the Bible and Qur'an wording side by side (KJV, Pickthall), how Muslims explain the difference with a check status, a strength label (direct conflict, different detail, addition only, depends on reading), the Scripture assessment, a manuscript test of taharif, and source links.
- Primary sources: 17 new entries (Ibn Kathir, Ma'arif al-Qur'an, al-Mizan, IslamQA, TheTorah.com, WikiIslam, al-Tabari tafsir and History).
- Not checked directly: al-Tabari's text; Genesis Rabbah, Pirke de-Rabbi Eliezer, Tanhuma, Testament of Solomon; several Muslim identifications marked verify.
