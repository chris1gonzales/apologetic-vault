# Veritas Reformata v3.42

Adds a "How this course teaches" note to the Study course (collapsible panel under the progress line).

- Each course feature is matched to the research it borrows from, with a badge and a link.
- Secondary = abstract or record read, full paper not opened (Roediger and Karpicke 2006; Cepeda and others 2006; Chi and others 1994; Dunlosky and others 2013; Bloom 1968 via record and summaries).
- Verify = course design choices that no study backs (shuffled options, track order).
- Lists what the course does not do yet: interleaving, adaptive spacing, confidence ratings, fresh gate questions on retry, and testing on learners.

Changed files: src/Curriculum.tsx, src/study.css, src/SourcesRegister.tsx, src/main.tsx, src/PassageFinder.tsx, package.json, README-v3.42.md.
