# Veritas Reformata v3.37

- New Phrase search page with separate Bible and Qur'an tabs. Found in the Scripture hub and the Islam & the Qur'an hub.
- Exact phrase first, ignoring capitals, punctuation, hyphens and curly quotes. If no verse has the phrase, verses with all the words in any order, then verses with most of the words, best first.
- Each match shows the reference and the verse with the matched words highlighted. Tap to open 1 to 10 verses either side (3 by default) inside the same chapter or surah.
- Bible: BSB or KJV; filter by Old or New Testament and by book. Qur'an: Pickthall; filter by surah; optional modern-wording matching (God/Allah, you/thee/thou, but/save) that changes matching only.
- Works offline from the stored corpus files. No new data files.
- Sources page: new row. BSB licence read at source; Pickthall and Tanzil terms Secondary only.
