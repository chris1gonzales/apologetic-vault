# Veritas Reformata v3.32

Three new entries on the Torah and Qur'anic Accounts page (Accounts compared):

1. **Hagar, Ishmael and Ishmael's marriage** (Genesis 16, 21, 25 against Bukhari 3364; Q 14:37). The Qur'an does not name Hagar. Genesis has an Egyptian wife (21:21); the hadith has two Jurhum wives (one divorced, one kept).
2. **From Abraham to Mecca: the journey.** Genesis keeps Abraham in Canaan and Ishmael in Paran; the Qur'an tells no journey; the hadith names Mecca but gives no route. The Muslim argument that Paran is the Hijaz is flagged "Verify" (not researched).
3. **Muhammad's line back to Ishmael.** Muslim scholars agree the line to 'Adnan and say what lies between 'Adnan and Ishmael is uncertain. Qedar and Nebayot are attested in north Arabia (Wadi Sirhan, Duma, Tayma). No source read links Quraysh to Kedar or Nebaioth outside later Islamic genealogies. Presented as a gap in the record, not a refutation.

Each entry has a new "Sources and checks" list with badges: Checked at source, Secondary only, Verify. Entries appear in global search. A row was added to the Sources register.
