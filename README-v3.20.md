# Veritas Reformata v3.20

Torah and Qur'anic Accounts expanded from 6 to 18 accounts.

- Grouped by part of Scripture: Torah, Prophets, Writings, Gospels, and accounts with no Bible parallel. Each is labelled shared account, conflict, or addition.
- New conflicts: Noah's son, Moses' adoptive mother, Haman, Aaron and the calf, Solomon, David, which son was offered, Saul/Talut, and the crucifixion (Gospels). Also the Zabur and the Psalms, the burning bush and Sinai, and Moses and al-Khidr.
- English text on both sides for every account: KJV and Pickthall (1930), both public domain, stored offline in src/tqTexts.ts. Excerpts only.
- Manuscript evidence per account for both sides. Anything uncertain is marked "verify".
- Button to open OT & Qur'an Manuscript Criticism.
